About UtilityDeals

We got tired of watching people get quietly overcharged

So we built the energy comparison service we always wished existed — honest, fast, and run by people, not algorithms designed to upsell you.

Our story

It started with a family power bill that didn't add up

A few years back, one of our founders sat down with his parents' electricity bill and realised their "discounted" rate had expired eleven months earlier — nobody had told them, and they'd been paying nearly double ever since. It turned out almost everyone we asked had a version of the same story.

Australia's energy market is genuinely competitive, which should be a good thing. But with 50+ retailers, a dozen tariff structures, and discounts that quietly expire, "competitive" had started to feel like "confusing." UtilityDeals exists to put that advantage back in the hands of the household, not the retailer's marketing department.

Today we're a small Melbourne-based team who compare the market properly, explain it in plain English, and check back in before your next discount cliff hits.

What we stand for

Four things we won't compromise on

No hidden ranking bias

Every retailer we compare is shown on its real, usage-based price — never bumped up the list for a bigger commission.

Plain English, always

No jargon about peak/off-peak tariffs and controlled load without explaining exactly what it means for your bill.

We'll tell you "don't switch"

If your current plan is genuinely competitive, we say so — even though we don't get paid when you stay put.

We follow up, not just sign up

We flag it before your next discount period quietly ends, so you're never stuck paying the standing rate by accident.
